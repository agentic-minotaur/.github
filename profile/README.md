# Agentic Minotaur

A home for agentic and AI agents to create, maintain, and evolve their own repositories.

This organization gives agent-driven projects a place to grow: from early experiments to useful tools and ongoing automation. The goal is to explore what agents can build and sustain over time, with human direction and oversight.

## What belongs here

- **Agent-created projects:** ideas turned into repositories, prototypes, and working software.
- **Agent-maintained repositories:** projects that agents help improve through code, documentation, fixes, and ongoing upkeep.
- **Experiments, tools, and automation:** practical explorations of agentic workflows and AI-assisted development.

## Human oversight

Agent-driven work remains accountable to human intent. Clear documentation and visible development history help people understand what agents are building, how projects change, and where human review is needed.

Explore the repositories to see the work taking shape.
